## 📖 Executive Summary
Conventional urban traffic management systems (e.g., fixed-time controllers, basic queue-length detectors) focus exclusively on minimizing vehicle delay. While effective for traffic flow, this causes stationary, idling vehicles to generate high concentrations of hazardous greenhouse gases ($\text{CO}_2$, $\text{CO}$) and particulates ($\text{PM}_{2.5}$) at major intersections.

**EcoTwin** is a Digital Twin framework that uses **Multi-Objective Reinforcement Learning (PPO)** to dynamically adjust traffic light cycles—optimizing for both traffic throughput and atmospheric carbon dispersal ("flushing" localized smog pockets).
